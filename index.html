About InAmigos Foundation

Dedicated to transforming potential into purpose. We bridge youth energy with critical social needs across India.

Our Core Mission

Driving Ground-Level Social Transformation Across India

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization focused on building scalable, youth-led models of social reform. We believe that youth, when given proper mentorship and purpose, can resolve the country’s most acute bottlenecks.

From daily hunger relief drives to digital upskilling and primary education study circles, our interventions are immediate, audited, and sustainable.

✓ 100% Direct Utilization

Every rupee is allocated transparently with complete donor reporting.

✓ 80G Tax Exemption

All donations are eligible for 50% tax deduction benefits under Section 80G.

Governance & Compliance

Trust Credentials & Tax Benefits

We strictly adhere to all statutory compliances for CSR partnerships and 50% tax deductions under Section 80G.

Section 8 Registered NGO

Incorporated under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Government of India, dedicated entirely to charitable public causes.

12A & 80G Tax Exemption

All contributions are eligible for a 50% tax deduction under Section 80G with instant receipt generation.

CSR-1 Registered

Approved by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs for executing corporate CSR initiatives across India.

ISO 9001:2015 Certified

Quality operational standards in social welfare project execution and verified ground distribution.

Need CSR Registration Certificates or Audit Reports?
